Recitation 3

Supply and Demand Equilibrium

Problem 1: Strawberry Market

In a small town, the market for fresh strawberries operates perfectly competitively. The demand and supply for strawberries in this town are governed by the following equations:

Demand Equation: \( Q_D = 60 - 5P \)
Supply Equation: \( Q_S = -20 + 10P \)

where \( Q_D \) and \( Q_S \) are the quantity demanded and supplied respectively, and \( P \) is the price of strawberries per pound.

(a) Equilibrium Price and Quantity

Determine the equilibrium price and quantity.

(b) Supply and Demand Graph

Draw and label the market equilibrium price and quantity as well as consumer surplus and producer surplus.

(c) Price Increase Scenario

Suppose a disease affects the strawberry crop, reducing the supply. The new supply equation is: \( Q_S = -40 + 10P \). Calculate the new equilibrium price and quantity, and illustrate the changes on your graph.

(d) Price Elasticity of Demand

If the price of strawberries increases from $4 to $5, calculate the price elasticity of demand using the difference method.

(e) Consumer and Producer Surplus

Calculate the consumer surplus and producer surplus at the initial equilibrium.

Demand Equation: \( Q_D = 250 - 25P \)
Supply Equation: \( Q_S = -30 + 20P \)

where \( Q_D \) and \( Q_S \) are the quantity demanded and supplied respectively, and \( P \) is the price of a notebook in dollars.

(a) Equilibrium Price and Quantity

Determine the equilibrium price and quantity.

(b) Supply and Demand Graph

Draw and label the market equilibrium price and quantity as well as consumer surplus and producer surplus.

(c) Price Elasticity of Demand

Calculate the price elasticity of demand using the difference method if the price decreases from $8 to $7.

(d) Consumer and Producer Surplus

Calculate the consumer surplus and producer surplus at the initial equilibrium.
